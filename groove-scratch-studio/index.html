— BARS

The grid is derived from BPM (4/4). Reset to source interval length for fractional bars, or choose 1, 2, or 4 whole bars.

Use DRAW TOOL to insert a scratch within a dragged rectangle (both vinyl and crossfader curves are generated). The curve is the exact sample position over time, rather than a velocity envelope. Dragging steeply backwards generates reverse audio. Horizontal lines hold a groove stationary (silence gated). Edit with or without 16th-note snapping; quantize writes the grid into node times.
