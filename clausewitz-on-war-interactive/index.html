Orientation: what the whole book is trying to do

One sentence: war is a political act carried out through violent collision, uncertainty, moral force, organization, chance, and limited means.

The usable modern reading is not “war is good”; it is “organized conflict cannot be understood as pure math, pure heroism, or pure policy. It is a coupled system.”
