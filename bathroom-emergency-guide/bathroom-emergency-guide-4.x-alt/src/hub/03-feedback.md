# Feedback

This guide is maintained by real humans who want it to actually help.
If something was wrong, missing, confusing, or saved your day — we'd like
to know.

## How to reach us

**Email:** [bathroom_emergency@fkr.dev](mailto:bathroom_emergency@fkr.dev)

**Online:** [be.fkr.dev](https://be.fkr.dev)

## What's useful to include

- Which subguide you were reading (the colour name on the cover works)
- What happened — even one sentence is enough
- Whether the advice worked, didn't work, or was missing entirely
- Anything that was confusing, wrong, or unhelpful

## What we do with feedback

Corrections and improvements go into the next revision. No personal data
is stored beyond what you choose to send. Feedback that identifies a
safety-critical error is prioritised immediately.

## For deployers

If you placed this guide in a bathroom and want to report on how it's
being received, or request customisation help, use the same address.
Include your deployment location if you'd like location-specific content
(local emergency numbers, language, climate hazards).
