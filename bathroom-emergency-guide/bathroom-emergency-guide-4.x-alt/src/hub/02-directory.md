# Subguide Directory

Eleven subguides. Each one is self-contained. Each one has its own colour, pattern,
and scope. Start with the one that matches your situation.

---

## calm — The Teal Book

![Calm mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_calm.png)

**Anxiety, panic, breathing, comfort, and exit strategies**

Breathing techniques, grounding, panic de-escalation, and the 90-second landing
sequence. Referenced by almost every other subguide — if in doubt, start here.

Accent: `#2E8B7A` | Pattern: wave

---

## ambulance — The Red Book

![Ambulance mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_ambulance.png)

**First aid, pain, triage, and vital signs**

You are the first responder because you are the only one here. Scene safety,
triage, bleeding, burns, fractures, vital observations, and when to call 112.

Accent: `#C23D2E` | Pattern: cross

---

## responsibility — The Amber Book

![Responsibility mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_responsibility.png)

**Harm, guilt, caregiving, and repair**

You caused trouble (or think you did). The five-step repair sequence, caregiving
stress, postpartum crisis, and the difference between guilt and shame.

Accent: `#D4880A` | Pattern: diamond

---

## safety — The Blue Book

![Safety mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_safety.png)

**Danger, coercion, shelter, and environmental hazards**

No safe place. Fire, gas, chemical, electrical, or interpersonal danger.
Securing the next safe hour and accessing refuge routes in Germany.

Accent: `#2563EB` | Pattern: shield

---

## zombie — The Olive Book

![Zombie mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_zombie.png)

**Survival, outages, community, and preparedness**

Power cuts, floods, heat, cold, water contamination, and groups improvising
governance. Half survival knowledge, half thought experiment.

Accent: `#4A6741` | Pattern: crosshatch

---

## support — The Indigo Book

![Support mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_support.png)

**Numbers, scripts, legal, and medical routes**

Germany quick-reference contact table. Crisis lines, social services, legal aid,
and what to say when calling a helpline for the first time.

Accent: `#6366F1` | Pattern: dots

---

## appendix — The Appendix

![Appendix mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_appendix.png)

**Formulas, flowcharts, fillable fields, and print card**

The master cross-reference, pocket card, decision flowcharts, and fillable
personal-data fields. Reference material, not a compulsory corridor.

Accent: `#B5763B` | Pattern: solid

---

## body — The Green Book

![Body mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_body.png)

**Skin, teeth, digestion, periods, allergies, and "is this normal?"**

Body signals, GI distress, the pain NRS, hydration, medication interactions,
and the recovery position. Your body is producing symptoms — this guide helps
you read them.

Accent: `#16A34A` | Pattern: pulse

---

## social — The Purple Book

![Social mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_social.png)

**Awkward moments, etiquette, hosting, and small-talk**

Absence management, communication templates, the mirror conversation, social
contexts, boundaries, and re-entry strategies. For when the problem is other
people (or your feelings about other people).

Accent: `#9333EA` | Pattern: speech

---

## disaster — The Orange Book

![Disaster mascot](build/diagrams/mascot_disaster.png)

**Earthquake, flood, storm, and extreme weather**

The bathroom as shelter, earthquake drop-cover-hold, severe weather, fire,
building emergencies, water safety, and recovery. The good news: the bathroom
is not a terrible place to ride out many emergencies.

Accent: `#EA580C` | Pattern: zigzag

---

## templates — The Grey Book

**Printable forms, observation logs, checklists, and fill-in sheets**

Ten forms derived from "write that down" tasks across all subguides:
observation log, pain record, call script, comfort inventory, local resource
directory, factual timeline, housing evidence pack, body scan record, feedback
form, and deployer notes.

Accent: `#6B7280` | Pattern: solid
