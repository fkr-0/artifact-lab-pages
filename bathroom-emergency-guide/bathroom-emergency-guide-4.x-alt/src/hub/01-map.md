# Navigation Map

This map shows the primary routing logic for the Bathroom Emergency Guide.
Follow the flowchart from your starting situation to the correct subguide.

![Master flowchart v2](build/diagrams/master_flowchart_v2.png)

---

## Quick routing table

| Situation | First stop | Subguide |
|---|---|---|
| Panic, anxiety, overwhelm | Calm — breathe first | calm |
| Physical injury, pain, medical symptoms | Triage and assess | ambulance |
| Body question ("is this normal?") | Body Owner's Manual | body |
| I am not safe here / danger present | Secure the next hour | safety |
| Major outage / infrastructure failure | Survival priorities | zombie |
| Natural disaster / extreme weather | Natural Disasters | disaster |
| I caused harm to someone | Responsibility sequence | responsibility |
| Awkward social situation | Social Field Guide | social |
| I need a professional or service | Numbers and scripts | support |
| I need the reference cards or flowcharts | Appendix | appendix |
| I need a printable form or checklist | Templates & Forms | templates |

---

## How the routing logic works

1. **Is there immediate danger to life?** → 112 first, then return here.
2. **Is the problem primarily psychological?** → calm first, then re-route.
3. **Is the problem primarily physical?** → ambulance for assessment.
4. **Is the problem a missing resource or external threat?** → safety or zombie.
5. **Do you need a number, a service, or a script?** → support.
