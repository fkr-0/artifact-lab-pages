# Templates & Forms — Write It Down So Your Brain Doesn't Have To

Every chapter in this guide eventually asks you to record, note, list, or fill
in something. This subguide collects those tasks in one place as printable
forms. Tear them out, photocopy them, or photograph them — they're yours.

::: {.infobox}

**How to use these forms**

- **Before a crisis:** fill in what you can (deployer info, comfort inventory,
  local contacts). Photocopy blanks for future use.
- **During a crisis:** use the observation log, pain record, or call script.
  Writing slows the panic and gives you data instead of dread.
- **After a crisis:** fill in the timeline, evidence pack, or feedback form.

:::

## Form 3: Emergency Call Script

*Referenced from: Support [supp.1.2]*

Fill in what you can. Read it to the dispatcher.

::: {.infobox}

**Script**

"I am at **________________________________** *(address, floor, door code)*.

The problem is **________________________________** *(one sentence)*.

It started **____________** *(time)*.

The person is **____________** *(awake / unresponsive)* and
**____________** *(breathing normally / not)*.

There is **____________** *(danger / no known danger)* at the scene.

My callback number is **________________________**.

Allergies: **________________________________**

Medications: **________________________________**"

:::
