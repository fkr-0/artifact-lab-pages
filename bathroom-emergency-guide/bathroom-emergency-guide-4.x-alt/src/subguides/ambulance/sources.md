## Sources

The following sources support content in the Self Ambulance Guide (first aid, triage,
bleeding, burns, fractures, vital signs, and escalation).

1. **gesund.bund.de.** "Nummern fuer den Notfall." Emergency number distinctions.
   https://gesund.bund.de/notfallnummern

7. **BBK.** "European emergency number 112."
   https://www.bbk.bund.de/SharedDocs/Kurzmeldungen/DE/2026/02/om-11-tag-notruf.html

24. **European Resuscitation Council.** Guidelines 2025. Adult basic life support,
    first aid, paediatric and newborn guidance, AED use.
    https://www.erc.edu/science-research/guidelines/guidelines-2025/guidelines-2025-english/

25. **ERC.** Guidelines 2025 for Everyone (layperson summary).
    https://www.erc.edu/media/p5ymaeej/gl2025_layperson_book_ipdf-v11-e.pdf

26. **German Red Cross.** Finding a person in an emergency.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/auffinden-einer-person/

27. **German Red Cross.** Severe bleeding: direct pressure, wound care, warmth.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/blutungen-und-blutstillung/blutungen/

28. **German Red Cross.** Burns and scalds: cooling, sterile covering.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/verbrennungen/

29. **German Red Cross.** Fractures: avoid movement, support the limb.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/knochenbruch/knochenbrueche/

30. **German Red Cross.** Chemical burns: self-protection, clothing removal, irrigation.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/veraetzungen/

31. **German Red Cross.** Hypothermia and frostbite: slow warming, no rubbing.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/erfrierungen-und-unterkuehlungen/

32. **German Red Cross.** Electrical injury first aid.
    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/stromschlag/

35. **Saver JL.** "Time is brain -- quantified." *Stroke* 37 (2006): 263–266.
    https://doi.org/10.1161/01.STR.0000196957.55928.ab

59. **da Silva RMFL.** "Syncope: epidemiology, etiology, and prognosis,"
    *Frontiers in Physiology* 5 (2014): 471.
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4258989/
