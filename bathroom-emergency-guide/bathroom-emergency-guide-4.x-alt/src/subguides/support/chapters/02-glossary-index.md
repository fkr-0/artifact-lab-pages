colour book sent you.

## Emergency numbers — expanded reference

::: {.infobox}

**When to use which number**

| Number | What it does | When to use | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **112** | Dispatches fire, rescue, ambulance | Life danger, fire, medical emergency, acute self-harm | 24/7 |
| **110** | Dispatches police | Active crime, violence, stalking, immediate threat | 24/7 |
| **116 117** | Connects to on-call doctor | Urgent illness outside practice hours, not life-threatening | 24/7 |
| **116 123** | TelefonSeelsorge | Psychological crisis, loneliness, suicidal thoughts | 24/7 |
| **116 016** | Violence against women helpline | DV, sexual violence, stalking, forced marriage — also for helpers | 24/7 |
| **0800 1239900** | Violence against men helpline | DV, violence against men | Published hours |
| **116 111** | Nummer gegen Kummer | Children and young people | Published hours |
| **115** | Public authority finder | "Which office handles this?" | Mon–Fri 08–18 |
| **Poison centre** | Regional poison information | Suspected poisoning, no immediate danger | Regional hours |

:::

## How the reference scheme works

Every section in this guide has a stable address in the form
`[book.chapter.section]`. The abbreviations are:

| Book | Abbreviation | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Calm Guide | calm | Teal |
| Self Ambulance | amb | Red |
| Responsibility | resp | Amber |
| Safety | safe | Blue |
| Zombie Guide | zomb | Olive |
| Support | supp | Indigo |
| Appendix | app | Copper |
| Body Manual | body | Green |
| Social Guide | soc | Purple |
| Natural Disasters | dis | Orange |
| Templates | tmpl | Grey |

Use these to point someone to a specific section: *"check [amb.1.7] for
wound care"* works whether they have the combined guide or just the Red Book.
