# Bathroom Emergency Guide 4.4.1 — Common Source Synthesis

Status: released
Base: `v4.3.1` at `03a5ee17a0111301871e95b3fa093aa3cc0d83b0`
Alternate input: untracked `src-alt/`, revision family `4.0.0-alt`
Prepared: 22 July 2026

## Purpose

4.4.1 synthesizes the strongest compatible material from the canonical 4.3.1
source and the alternate source tree without replacing the safer architecture.
The result keeps the Small-Room Observatory, current routing, accessibility,
source freshness, density checks, and graph/subguide plans while adding a more
operational household-continuity and group-handoff layer.

## Canonical material retained

- curiosity-first opening and single master emergency gate;
- B–F identity plus dedicated Situation G and Situation H;
- current de-DE route, locale, accessibility, and source-freshness registries;
- bounded quantitative evidence and explicit practical limits;
- A4, A4/2, large-print, color, and monochrome parity;
- graph-oriented subguide, visualization, and source-localization architecture.

## Alternate material accepted

- explicit ownership and backup rather than anonymous “someone should” tasks;
- a first-meeting structure with short briefings, task owners, and review times;
- continuity framed as functions: information, air, care/power, water,
  temperature/shelter, food, sanitation, and access/transport;
- practical human-factors language around uncertainty, dissent, confidence, and
  named assignments;
- destigmatizing safe-place language;
- selected voice improvements that do not sit between a red flag and its action;
- the hub/booklet idea only where it agrees with the later graph/subguide plan.

## Alternate material rejected or deferred

- the shorter route-first opening, reduced evidence appendix, abbreviated source
  chapter, and replacement release history;
- duplicated per-subguide source files rather than generated local source views;
- emergency gates repeated inside every master-guide section;
- unsafe or unsupported material on insects, scavenging, ash handwashing,
  improvised sanitation distances, generic fever thresholds, the “milk trick,”
  lightning 30/30 guidance, and action-oriented Beaufort scales;
- wilderness trace-reading claims, personal survival durations, simplified
  developmental timelines, and compressed legal decision trees;
- a ten-book color-name system that conflicts with stable code + pattern + glyph
  identities;
- immediate implementation of Body, Social, and Natural Disaster guides before
  source ownership and graph placement are reviewed.
