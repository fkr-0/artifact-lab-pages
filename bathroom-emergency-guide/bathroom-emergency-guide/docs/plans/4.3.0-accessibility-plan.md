# Bathroom Emergency Guide 4.3.0 — Locale, Accessibility, and Safe-Place Plan

Version 4.3.0 turns “no safe place” and “accessibility” from editorial intentions
into routes, data, outputs, and tests.

## Safe-place route split

The old G branch combined several failures that need different services. The
4.3.0 implementation separates:

1. person, violence, coercion, or active threat;
2. no weather-safe roof tonight;
3. a place that fails access or essential-care requirements;
4. a physically safe place made unworkable by social or internal crisis.

Each route must expose action, backup, escalation, destination, service keys,
and reviewed sources. A national number may identify a local authority; it must
not be described as guaranteeing accommodation.

## Source freshness

Operational sources and locale records carry review dates and maximum ages.
Validation must:

- reject malformed or future review dates;
- warn when a source approaches its review window;
- fail when an operational source exceeds its own maximum age;
- support `GUIDE_AS_OF=YYYY-MM-DD` for deterministic future/staleness tests;
- keep national services separate from locally supplied accommodation,
  transport, and after-hours values.

## Communication access profiles

The structured registry covers:

- blind and low-vision access;
- deaf and hard-of-hearing access;
- speech and language access;
- cognitive overload and processing time;
- mobility, fatigue, pain, transfer, and equipment;
- sensory, panic, and neurodivergent access.

Every profile needs a barrier description, immediate adaptation, handoff, and
failure-escalation condition. The urgency threshold remains unchanged.

## Large-print edition

Add color and monochrome A4 large-print editions with:

- body type at the reviewed 13.25 pt print root;
- single-column route cards;
- larger headings and emergency numbers;
- bounded table, code, formula, and figure typography;
- a visible reminder that the complete text route follows each figure;
- tagged PDF output with color/monochrome text parity.

- existing A4/2 layout parity.

## Release acceptance

A 4.3.0 candidate is acceptable when:

- Situation G exists as a dedicated chapter and the old inline branch is only a
  router;
- the four structured safe-place routes and six access profiles validate;
- current de-DE national numbers are source-linked and local values remain
  visibly local;
- a simulated stale validation date fails for at least one operational source;
- standard A4, A4/2, and large-print color/mono builds all complete;
- all PDFs are tagged, contain no extracted blank pages, and preserve their
  color/monochrome text;
- the roadmap records unfinished locale expansion rather than claiming a
  universal shelter directory.
