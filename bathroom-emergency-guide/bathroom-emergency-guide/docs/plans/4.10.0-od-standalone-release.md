# 4.10.0 — O and D standalone release plan

## Scope

Release the existing canonical **O — Small-Room Observatory** and **D — Threat and Safe Place** material as standalone A4, A4/2, and large-print colour/monochrome families without duplicating reader prose.

## Canonical extraction

- **O** extracts `01-how-to-use.md`. Its standalone cover and page-zero emergency gate are generated by the shared subguide builder; the master document cover is not copied, preventing duplicate emergency gates.
- **D** extracts the D-owned section from `03-situations-b-g.md` followed by the complete `03g-safe-place-routing.md` chapter.
- Both families retain canonical stable heading IDs, local source replacement, graph identity, complete text handoffs, and generated Sources and limits.

## Release thresholds

### O

- at least 11 local source records;
- at least four owned reader visuals;
- exactly one standalone emergency gate;
- required Observatory, first-90-seconds, interoception, and bounded-observation sections;
- no foreign B/C/D/H owned headings in the canonical content region.

### D

- at least eight local operational source records;
- at least four owned reader visuals;
- exactly one standalone emergency gate;
- required threat-clock, safe-place confirmation, G1/G3, communication-access, and handoff sections;
- no foreign B/C/E/F owned headings in the canonical content region.

## Matrix and publication boundary

- Expand the released set from B/C/H/P/T/R to O/B/C/D/H/P/T/R.
- Expand standalone PDFs from 36 to 48, preserving three layouts and two print modes per family.
- Update graph hub, landing page, download catalogue, release metadata, CI-facing validators, changelog, README, roadmap, and in-guide version history.
- Keep A and Z master-only.
- Build, test, commit, and create an annotated local tag only after exact-revision verification.
- Do not push, publish, deploy, or run the prepared Pages deployment workflow.

## Evidence boundary

Build success establishes extraction integrity, source completeness, semantic parity, tagged PDF output, geometry, overflow, accessibility checks, browser packaging, and artifact hashes. It does not establish real-reader comprehension, safe-place availability, current local service capacity, or successful use in an actual crisis.
