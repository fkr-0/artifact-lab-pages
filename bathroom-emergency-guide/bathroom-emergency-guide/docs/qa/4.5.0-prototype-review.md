# 4.5.0 prototype and release-candidate review

Review date: 2026-07-23
Scope: engineering visual, content, print, and reproducibility review of the
nine-node hub plus the B/H standalone vertical slices.

## Result

The 4.5.0 candidate is suitable for release staging. The master remains the
canonical full guide; B and H now detach into source-complete, graph-oriented
standalone families without copying reader prose. All declared validators pass.

This was an engineering review, not a blinded usability study. It does not claim
a formal ten-second orientation time, comprehension score, or clinical outcome.
Those are useful follow-up studies, not release-candidate evidence invented after
the fact.

## Grouping and orientation decision

Two grouping prototypes were compared in
`build/qa/subguides/subguide_grouping_comparison.png`. The selected design keeps
a quiet nine-node orientation spine:

- O — Small-Room Observatory
- B — Alarm and Calm
- C — Body and First Aid
- H — Air, Smell, and Environment
- A — Responsibility and Care
- D — Threat and Safe Place
- Z — Outage and Continuity
- P — Professional Support
- R — Reference

Candidate satellites remain future graph extensions. Showing them in the main
hub now made route choice look like a curriculum rather than help. Direct B/H
links remain available, and the danger instruction stays outside the graph:
**Immediate danger → 112.**

The final overview at `build/diagrams/subguide_graph_overview.png` was inspected
after increasing its lower margin. The danger note no longer collides with P or
R. The graph is orientation, not a queue before emergency help.

## Identity review

The contact sheet at
`build/qa/subguides/subguide_identity_contact_sheet.png` shows all nine frozen
identities. Each combines a letter code, title, glyph, pattern, and colour.
Patterns do not sit behind body text. In monochrome, code, pattern, glyph, and
title remain; colour is supplementary.

Observed result: the identities are visually distinct in the engineering review
at A4 and A4/2 scale. A blinded recognition study remains unperformed and is not
claimed.

## Hub review

The first screen review exposed an implementation defect: the no-TOC hub still
reserved the master guide's TOC column, compressing the route cards. The shared
template now adds a `no-toc` body state, hides the inactive TOC control, and gives
the hub a single 992 px content column. The route grid uses three, two, or one
column according to viewport width.

Final checks:

- nine route cards are present in keyboard-readable document order;
- B and H expose direct standalone actions;
- the complete text graph remains available without interpreting SVG lines;
- the overview image, identity legend, and directory agree with the registry;
- narrow layouts collapse to one column instead of shrinking type.

## B — Alarm and Calm

The pilot combines concise action flow with explicitly bounded science:

- four-channel alarm map: body, environment, thought, and alarm;
- overload-control map separating reduce, support, and offload actions;
- conceptual load model `L = I + E + S` and headroom `H = K - L`;
- breathing-technique comparison and randomized-trial evidence map;
- six generated local sources and a final graph handoff.

The load/headroom model is labeled as a teaching model, not a measured clinical
scale or theorem about a person. It supports a practical rule: when load exceeds
available capacity, reduce inputs, add support, or offload tasks before demanding
more performance.

## Reproducibility and validation evidence

`npm test` passed on the current checkout after the final graph adjustment. The
suite covers:

- route and continuity registries;
- subguide identities, reciprocal graph edges, pattern assets, and hub outputs;
- Vega-Lite provenance, fallbacks, and monochrome encodings;
- illustration coverage;
- master/standalone semantic and source parity;
- guide safety and evidence invariants;
- A4/2 geometry and collision checks;
- A4 density and colour/mono parity;
- accessibility, tagging, alternate text, and large-print checks;
- boxed, table, preformatted-text, quotation, and figure overflow.

Master page counts are 99 A4, 95 A4/2, and 150 large print, with exact
colour/monochrome page-count parity. Twelve tagged B/H standalone PDFs are in the
release matrix. The canonical inventory contains 52 chapter footnotes and 75
global source-note sections with zero unresolved references.
